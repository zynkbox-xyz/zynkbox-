Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Welcome to Zynkbox. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to ensure the best possible experience:

  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous data about how you interact with our site (e.g., most popular recipes).
  • Contact Data: If you use our contact form, we collect your name and email to respond to your inquiry.
  • Cookies: We use basic cookies to remember your preferences.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service.
  • To respond to your contact requests.
  • To analyze site traffic and improve our content.

3. Data Sharing

Zynkbox does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Please contact us at hello@zynkbox.xyz to make such requests.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Truffle-Infused
Wild Mushroom Risotto

Prep: 20 mins Cook: 45 mins Difficulty: Advanced Yield: 4 Servings

The secret to a truly world-class risotto is not found in the ingredients alone, but in the patient, rhythmic dance between the rice and the broth. In this guide, we explore the alchemy of the Carnaroli grain—the king of risotto rice—and how it transforms into a creamy, opulent canvas for the earthiest flavors of the forest.

Truffles are more than just a luxury; they are a sensory experience. By infusing our wild mushroom blend with high-grade black truffle oil and finishing with fresh shavings, we create a dish that is both grounding and ethereal.

"Cooking is not just about following a recipe; it is about listening to the ingredients and knowing exactly when the heat has done its work."

The Culinary Philosophy

To understand the Risotto, one must understand the mantecatura. This is the final, vigorous beating of cold butter and aged Parmesan into the rice after it has been removed from the heat. It is this step that creates the emulsion—the signature creaminess that separates a professional risotto from a home-cooked version.

We utilize a blend of Porcini, Chanterelles, and Shiitake mushrooms, each bringing a different tonal quality to the dish. The Porcini provides the deep bass, the Chanterelles a bright apricot note, and the Shiitake a subtle, savory hum.

The Essentials

  • Carnaroli Rice (Premium) 400g
  • Wild Mushroom Blend (Fresh) 500g
  • Shallots, finely diced 2 large
  • Dry White Wine (Pinot Grigio) 150ml
  • Organic Chicken or Vegetable Stock 1.5L
  • Unsalted European Butter (Cold) 80g
  • Parmigiano-Reggiano (24-month aged) 100g
  • Black Truffle Oil (First Press) 15ml
  • Fresh Black Truffle (for garnish) 10g
  • Fresh Thyme & Flat Leaf Parsley 1 bunch each

The Method

01

The Mushroom Base

Sauté the wild mushroom blend in a heavy-bottomed pan with a knob of butter and a sprig of thyme until golden brown. Set aside half for the final garnish. The mushrooms should be caramelized, not steamed, to preserve their concentrated essence.

02

The Toasting (Tostatura)

In a large pot, sweat the shallots in butter until translucent. Add the rice and toast it over medium heat for 2-3 minutes. You are looking for the edges of the grains to become translucent while the center remains white. This locks in the starch and ensures the grain doesn't break.

03

The Deglaze

Pour in the white wine. The hiss is the sound of flavor activation. Stir constantly until the wine has been fully absorbed, leaving behind only its bright, acidic skeleton.

04

The Patient Addition

Add the warm stock one ladle at a time. Stir gently but constantly. Do not drown the rice; wait until each ladle is absorbed before adding the next. This friction releases the starch, creating the creamy sauce.

Perfect Pairings

To complement the earthy depth of the truffle, we recommend a full-bodied Nebbiolo or a refined Chardonnay. The acidity of the wine cuts through the richness of the butter and cheese, resetting the palate for every bite.

Chef's Secret Tips

The Temperature Secret: Always keep your stock at a low simmer on the burner next to your risotto. Adding cold stock shocks the rice and ruins the starch release.

The Final Touch: Stir in the truffle oil only at the very end. Heat destroys the volatile aromatic compounds of the truffle; adding it during the mantecatura preserves the scent.
